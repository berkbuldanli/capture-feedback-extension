# Privacy Policy — Quick Capture

_Last updated: 2026-07-07_

Quick Capture is designed to be private. This policy explains, in plain
language, what the extension does and does not do with your information.

## The short version

**Quick Capture does not collect, transmit, or share any of your data.**
Everything you save stays on your own device, inside your browser's local
storage. There are no accounts, no servers, and no analytics.

## What data the extension stores

When you save a card, Quick Capture stores the following **locally in your
browser** (using the `storage.local` API):

- The page title, URL, and the date/time you saved it
- Any text you highlighted on the page
- Notes and tags you add
- The collection you assign
- An optional screenshot of the page (only if you enable the 📷 option)
- Your settings (theme and whether screenshots are on)

## Where your data goes

Nowhere. The data:

- **is stored only on the device where you use the extension;**
- **is never sent to us or to any third party;**
- **is not used for advertising, tracking, or profiling.**

The extension makes **no network requests** to any external server.

## Permissions

Quick Capture requests the minimum permissions needed to work:

- **storage / unlimitedStorage** — to save your cards locally.
- **activeTab / scripting** — to read the current tab's title, URL, selected
  text, and to take a screenshot, **only at the moment you invoke the
  extension** (clicking the icon, using the shortcut, or the right-click menu).
- **contextMenus** — to add the "Save to Quick Capture" right-click item.

The extension does **not** request access to your browsing history or to all
websites, and it does not run in the background on the pages you visit.

## Your control over your data

- You can delete any card, or all cards, at any time from the extension.
- You can export your data to a file, and import it back, at any time.
- Removing/uninstalling the extension deletes its stored data from your browser.

## Children's privacy

Quick Capture is a general-purpose productivity tool and does not knowingly
collect any information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same
location with a new "Last updated" date.
