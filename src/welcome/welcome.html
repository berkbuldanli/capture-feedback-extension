Welcome to Quick Capture

Your lightweight research notebook for the web. Save any page as a tagged card — with notes, screenshots, and collections — all stored privately in your browser.

Three ways to capture

🖱️

Click the icon

Open the toolbar popup, optionally add tags, a note, and a collection, then hit Save this page.

✏️

Right-click a selection

Highlight text on any page, right-click, and choose Save to Quick Capture — the quote is saved too.

⌨️

Keyboard shortcut

Press Alt+Shift+S on any page to save it instantly, without opening anything.

What you can do

  • Organise with tags and named collections
  • Add your own notes and a page screenshot
  • Search, filter, sort, and pin the important ones
  • Copy as Markdown to paste into Notion, Docs, or Slack
  • Bulk-manage many cards at once from the dashboard
  • Export / import everything as a JSON file for backup

🔒 Private by design

Everything stays in your browser's local storage. There are no accounts, no servers, and nothing is ever sent anywhere. Your captures are yours.

Tip: pin the Quick Capture icon to your toolbar (click the 🧩 puzzle icon → pin) so it's always one click away.
